Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how the ComLab Facilities Management System, used by the College of Computer Studies at Mindoro State University, handles information when you use this website.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password. Passwords are stored as hashes, not as plain text.
  • Student profile information: Student ID, program, year, section, campus, college or institute, and any profile photo you choose to upload.
  • Service records: information needed to provide laboratory services, such as attendance, equipment borrowing, requests, and reports.
  • Security and activity records: sign-in sessions, email-verification and password-reset codes, and audit records used to secure and operate the system.

Google sign-in and email

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides ComLab with information such as your verified email address and profile name so we can authenticate you and match your ComLab account. ComLab does not receive your Google password.

ComLab uses an email delivery provider to send account-verification and password-reset messages to the address you provide. Those messages may include a temporary verification code.

How information is used and shared

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide laboratory and student services, manage attendance and equipment records, respond to requests, and maintain system integrity. Information may be available to authorized university personnel according to their roles and operational responsibilities.

We do not sell personal information. Google processes information when you use Google sign-in, and service providers that support email delivery, hosting, or database operations may process information as needed to provide the system.

Cookies and security

ComLab uses necessary session cookies to keep you signed in and protect your session. We use reasonable safeguards, including hashed passwords and role-based access controls, to help protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and your choices

Account and profile information is kept while your account is active. Attendance, borrowing, security, and other official school records may be retained according to applicable university record-retention policies, including after an account is closed.

For questions, corrections, or account-related requests, contact markjamesrhamzel@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained under university policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the system or its practices change. The latest version and effective date will be published on this page.
